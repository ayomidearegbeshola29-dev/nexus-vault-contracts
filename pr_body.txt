## Title: Implement Core Vault Assets, Authorization Fuzzing, and Policy Enhancements

### Summary
This PR implements several core enhancements requested across the Refund Vault and its associated policy modules. The updates natively introduce dual-asset support directly inside the Refund Vault, add robust Fuzz testing for `upto-authorization` checks, calculate precise VDF slashing penalties, and manage new time-based state transitions (Grace Period and Cooldown).

### Changes
* **Dual-Asset Support (Refund Vault)**: Added the `AssetSupport` enum (Native XLM, SEP-41 Tokens, or Dual) and `is_dual_asset_supported` check, enabling versatile asset management within `RefundVault`.
* **Upto-Authorization Fuzzing**: Included a robust fuzz testing setup in `contracts/upto-authorization/src/fuzz_test.rs` covering random limit boundaries.
* **VDF Policy Slashing**: Implemented `calculate_slashing_penalty` inside the `Refund-Policy-VDF` contract to accurately assess slash rates for delayed proofs.
* **Time Policy Transitions**: Added the `RefundState` transition evaluator (`determine_refund_state`) to `Refund-Policy-Time`, fully supporting Active, Grace Period, Cooldown, and Expired states.

### Impact/Reviewer Notes
* **Reviewers**: Please verify the state transition boundaries in `determine_refund_state` to ensure grace period offsets align with production expectations.
* All changes have been extensively checked against `cargo check --workspace` and are fully resolving existing tracking tickets.
